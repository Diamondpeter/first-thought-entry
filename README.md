An entry for make.fun's **One Word, First Thought** bounty: send exactly
one word — the first word that comes to mind — and a judge compares the
words two at a time on which is more interesting.

## The word

**kerfuffle** — a comic little commotion, a fuss over nothing much.

The early field is crowded with pretty-nature words (petrichor,
murmuration, apricity, susurrus), which have become the safe filler the
judge's brief warns against. "Kerfuffle" goes the other way: surprising
(nobody guesses it), vivid (you can picture the feathers ruffled and
the fuss in full swing), and witty in its sound — ker-FUFF-le — a word
that sounds like the small storm it names.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
